Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 21, 2026

Family Planner (the "Homestead Family Hub") is a private, personal app used by one family to keep a shared calendar. It is not offered to the public and has no commercial purpose.

What Google data the app uses

  • Gmail (read only): the app reads email messages and attachments in connected Google accounts to find dated family events such as school notices, appointments, and travel bookings.
  • Google Calendar: the app creates and updates events on a connected calendar once a family member approves them.

How the data is used

Email text and attachments that appear to contain events are stored in the family's private database (hosted on Supabase) and sent to an AI service (Anthropic) solely to extract event details. Extracted events are shown to family members for approval. Nothing is sold, shared with advertisers, or used for any purpose other than building the family calendar.

Who can see it

Only signed-in members of the family household. Access is protected by login and database row-level security.

Retention and deletion

Stored emails and documents can be deleted by any household member from within the app at any time. Disconnecting a Google account from the app revokes the app's access to that account. You can also revoke access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Limited Use disclosure

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
